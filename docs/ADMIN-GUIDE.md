# How to update the website

A short guide for editing the Global Place Partners website. You don't need any technical knowledge: everything is done in the admin area with ordinary forms.

Open the admin by adding **/admin** to the website address, for example `https://globalplacepartners.com/admin`.

![The sign-in screen](images/admin-login.png)

---

## 1. Signing in

1. Go to the **/admin** address.
2. Type your password and press **Sign in**.
   - **Main admin:** leave the email box empty and use the main password.
   - **Editor:** type the email address and password you were given.
3. You stay signed in for the day. Press **Sign out** (top right) when you finish, especially on a shared computer.

If it says "Invalid password", check you're using the right box: main admins leave the email empty.

---

## 2. Saving and publishing (read this first)

Your changes are **not** visible to visitors until you publish them. There are two steps:

1. **Save draft** keeps your changes safe, but only you can see them.
2. **Publish** makes the saved draft live for everyone.

The buttons are always at the top right.

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Save draft** | Keeps your changes. Visitors see nothing yet. |
| **Preview** | Opens the site in a new tab so you can check your work before it goes live. Press Save draft first. |
| **Publish** | Makes the site live for everyone. |
| **Sign out** | Ends your session. |

**Some things go live the moment you save them** and don't need Publish:

- LinkedIn posts
- Insights articles
- Uploaded pictures
- Editor accounts

Everything else (page text, people, the menu, colours, the contact form) waits for **Publish**.

> **Tip:** there is no warning if you close the tab. Always press **Save draft** before you leave or sign out, or your latest edits are lost.

---

## 3. Editing text on a page

![The pages screen](images/admin-pages.png)

1. Press the **pages** tab.
2. In the left column, click the page you want, for example **Home**.
3. Under the page details is the list of sections, top to bottom, in the order they appear on the page. Click the section whose text you want to change.
4. Edit the text in the boxes that appear:
   - **Eyebrow** is the small label above the heading.
   - **Heading** is the main heading.
   - **Supporting line** and **Body** are the text underneath.
5. Press **Save draft**, then **Preview** to check it looks right.
6. Press **Publish** when you're happy.

**A few more things you can do here:**

- **Move a section** up or down the page with the **Up** and **Down** buttons.
- **Hide a section** without deleting it by ticking **Hide section**.
- **Add a section** with the drop-down and the **Add section** button. It appears at the bottom; move it with Up and Down.
- **Remove a section** with its **Delete section** button.
- **Hide a whole page** by ticking **Hidden** next to the page title.

### The numbers on the home page

The row of figures near the top of the home page currently shows **[Stat]** as a stand-in, because the real numbers haven't been added yet. To finish it:

1. pages → **Home** → click the **stats** section.
2. Replace each **[Stat]** with the real figure, and put what it means in the line underneath.
3. Save draft, check it in Preview, then Publish.

If you don't have figures yet, tick **Hide section** on that section instead, so visitors don't see the stand-ins.

## 4. People

![The people screen](images/admin-people.png)

Press the **people** tab. Everyone is listed on the left under their group: **Founding Partners**, **Principals** and **Senior Associates**. Click a name to edit them.

### Add someone

1. Press **Add employee**.
2. Fill in their **Name**, **Role** (shown above the name) and **Group**.
3. Add a **Specialism line** (the short line under the name) and a **Summary** (the few lines on their card). If you leave the summary empty, the first paragraph of their biography is used.
4. Write their **Full biography**. This is what visitors see in the pop-up when they click the person. Leave a blank line between paragraphs. If you leave it empty, their card won't have a "Read full biography" link.
5. Paste their **LinkedIn** address if they have one.
6. Add their photo (see below).
7. Press **Save draft**, check the People page in **Preview**, then **Publish**.

### Change someone's details

1. Click their name in the left column.
2. Edit the boxes.
3. Save draft, then Publish.

### Change their photo

1. Click their name.
2. Press **Upload a photo** and choose the picture from your computer. A square, head-and-shoulders photo works best. The website gives every portrait the same look, so photos don't all need the same background.
3. Use the three sliders to get the face in the right place: **Crop horizontal**, **Crop vertical** and **Crop zoom**.
4. Fill in **Portrait description**, for example "Portrait of Jane Smith". This helps people using screen readers.
5. Save draft, then Publish.

### Change the order

Each group is ordered separately. Either:

- type a number in **Order within the group** (1 is first), or
- use the **Up** and **Down** buttons next to their name.

Then Save draft and Publish.

### Hide or remove someone

- **Temporarily:** tick **Hide from the site**. They stay in the admin but disappear from the website.
- **For good:** press **Delete employee** and confirm. They're removed when you Publish.

## 5. Adding a LinkedIn post

![The LinkedIn screen](images/admin-linkedin.png)

LinkedIn posts show on the home page and the Insights page. They go live as soon as you save them.

1. Press the **linkedin** tab, then **Add LinkedIn post**.
2. **Post link:** open the post on LinkedIn, copy the address from your browser's address bar, and paste it into **Post URL**.
3. **The embedded post (optional, but nicest).** This shows the real LinkedIn post on the site:
   1. On LinkedIn, open the post and click the **⋯** (three dots).
   2. Choose **Embed this post**. LinkedIn only offers this for public posts.
   3. You'll see a box of code. Find the part that starts with `https://www.linkedin.com/embed/` and copy just that address, up to the closing quote mark.
   4. Paste it into **Embed URL**.
4. If you don't add an embed, fill in the **Title** and **Excerpt** instead, and the site shows a simple card that links to LinkedIn.
5. Set the **Date** (newer dates appear first) and the **Author**.
6. Press **Add post**. It's live straight away. Check the home page to see it.

**Later on:** click the post in the left list to edit it, tick **Hide from the site** to take it down without deleting it, or press **Delete post** to remove it.

---

## 6. Changing pictures

1. Press the **media** tab.
2. Type a short description of the picture, then press **Upload image** and choose the file. It's available straight away.
3. Go to the page or person that should use it:
   - For a page section: **pages** → the page → the section → **Or choose from the library**.
   - For a person: **people** → their name → **Upload a photo**.
4. Add a description wherever you see **Image description** or **Portrait description**.
5. Save draft and Publish.

To swap a picture that's already used, find it in the **media** tab, choose a new file under **Replace file** and press **Update image**. It updates everywhere that picture appears.

---

## 7. Undoing a change

Made a mistake and already published it? You can go back.

1. Press the **history** tab.
2. You'll see a list of every time the site was published, newest first.
3. A main admin can press **Restore** next to an earlier version.

**Be careful:** Restore puts that older version live immediately, and it also replaces whatever you currently have in your draft. If you're unsure, fix the mistake by hand and publish again instead.

---

## 8. Quick tips

- **Save before you leave.** Press Save draft before closing the tab or signing out.
- **Preview before you publish.** Save draft, open Preview, and check on your phone as well as your computer.
- **One small publish is safer than a big one.** Publish as you go so it's easy to undo.
- **Hiding is gentler than deleting.** Use Hide section or Hide from the site when you might want something back.
- **Leave a blank line between paragraphs** in biographies and page text, or they'll run together.
- **Describe your pictures.** A short description helps visitors who can't see them.
- **Stuck?** The most common fix is: Save draft, then Publish, then refresh the page.

### The other tabs, briefly

- **insights**: articles for the Insights page. These go live when you save them.
- **navigation**: the menu links.
- **brand**: the logo, colours and footer text.
- **form**: the wording of the contact form.
- **access**: main admins can add or remove editor logins here.
